# Sprint 1 Plan

- **My user:** Some of my roommates.
- **Their problem:** Constantly tracking macros and fitness is difficult, and they need a solution that makes the process more streamlined.
- **The system:** It lets the user input activity, calories burned, and food (with a photo-to-calorie option) to make a progress chart showing a calorie deficit for a cut or a calorie surplus for a bulk.
- **Working alone or in a pair:** Working alone.

**Goal:**
- I will be able to synthesize exactly what I need to do to create this app.
- I will be able to take steps to constantly keep improving it.
- I will do this by making a roadmap of the steps and features I will need to add.

**Why this:**
- It offers the most convenient solution to the user's problem.
- It will also help them improve.

**Done looks like:** A grader can see a comparison of the time it takes in my app versus other methods, like writing things down in the notes app.

**Predicted difficulty:** 4
